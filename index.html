Why we're doing this

Sunrise Day Camp gives kids with cancer a summer to just be kids.

Sunrise Day Camp Greater Philadelphia welcomes children living with cancer, and their siblings, for a summer full of friends, laughs, and adventure. Our raffle brings that mission to Lawrenceville.

A camp for kids

Sunrise is a place where children facing cancer and their siblings can swim, play, make friends, and feel like themselves.

Teens lead the way

TTLC is the teen leadership program of Sunrise Greater Philadelphia. This raffle is powered by students who care.

Your $2 matters

One $2 ticket gives you a chance to win and helps Sunrise keep camp running. Buy one ticket or a handful, and every one counts.

How it works

Four easy steps to a brighter summer.

  1. 1

    Grab tickets

    Find us at lunch, or buy online. Tickets are $2 each, with no limit on how many you can buy.

  2. 2

    Pick where it goes

    Send your gift to Sunrise Greater Philadelphia, a Sunrise camp near your hometown, or Sunrise overall.

  3. 3

    Tell a friend

    Share the page. The more people who know about Sunrise, the more kids we can help.

  4. 4

    Win & celebrate

    Winners are drawn on raffle day: date coming soon. Everyone wins by supporting camp.

Our story

A TTLC teen, a big idea, and a school full of friends.

“

It started with a simple idea: a lunchtime raffle where anyone can buy as many tickets as they want for $2, and every cent goes to Sunrise.

A Lawrenceville student and TTLC member wanted to introduce friends and classmates to the camp that means so much to them. The raffle is the first step. Next year, we hope to grow it into SunriseWALKS at Lawrenceville.
